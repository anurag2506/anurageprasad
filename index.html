Research

Publication

Accepted · 2026

Modified TSMixer for synchrophasor prediction under cyber attacks

IEEE Transactions on Industry Applications · Co-author

Power grids rely on PMU sensors to report their state many times a second. When an attacker floods or blocks those feeds, operators lose visibility. This work predicts the missing readings accurately enough to keep monitoring running, using a TSMixer model with window-based normalization so it works on live streams.

Read the paper on IEEE Xplore
~18×lower error than TSMixer baseline
27denial-of-service scenarios, 1–14 min
<1%percentage error across all attacks
6 msper prediction, 0.66 MB model
